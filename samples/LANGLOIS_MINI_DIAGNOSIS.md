# Free 3-Point Mini-Diagnosis — Langlois Electric

Public sources checked 2026-10-24:
- https://langloiselectric.ca/
- https://langloiselectric.ca/contact/

## 1. Fix the visible placeholder contact address
The homepage currently exposes “Contact Us (info@example.com)” while the footer/contact page lists info@langloiselectric.ca.

Why it matters:
A placeholder email on a high-intent service page can reduce trust and can send prospects to the wrong action.

Fix:
Replace every placeholder reference with the live contact route and make the primary CTA consistently “Request Estimate.”

## 2. Unify the conversion path
The site uses Request Estimate, Contact Us, phone, and a membership CTA.

Fix:
On service pages, choose one primary conversion action (“Request Estimate”) and keep phone as the secondary action. Membership can remain lower in the page hierarchy.

## 3. Tighten trust proof near the first CTA
The homepage communicates experience and Métis ownership, but those proof points appear after the first request-estimate decision.

Fix:
Place a compact proof line immediately beside/under the first CTA, e.g.:
“15+ years experience · Upfront pricing · Guaranteed workmanship · Saskatoon, Warman & Martensville.”
