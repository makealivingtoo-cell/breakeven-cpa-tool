# Delivery SOP — Lead Recovery Snapshot

## Intake required
1. Business name
2. Website
3. Primary service
4. Primary city/service area
5. Main inquiry channel (phone/form/text/email)
6. Optional: monthly leads, average job value, approximate close rate

Do not require access to CRM, analytics, email, or call recordings for the base C$99 audit.

## 48-hour delivery checklist
1. Review homepage above-the-fold clarity: service, market, trust, CTA.
2. Review mobile inquiry path and number of steps to contact.
3. Review service-area and service-page clarity.
4. Review trust proof: reviews, credentials, guarantees, project examples, response expectations.
5. Review local discoverability using public search/profile signals.
6. Review AI-answer readiness: explicit services, locations, FAQs, structured factual copy, contact details.
7. Identify 5 highest-impact observable leaks. Label assumptions explicitly.
8. Draft:
   - 3 improved CTA variants
   - 1 homepage hero rewrite
   - 3-message quote follow-up sequence
   - 30-day prioritized plan
9. Perform factuality check: no invented analytics, revenue, rankings, or customer behavior.
10. Deliver PDF/document + concise email summary.

## Refund rule
If scope cannot be completed within 48 hours after complete intake, contact the customer immediately and offer either a new mutually accepted deadline or a full refund. No silent delays.

## Capacity rule
Maximum 5 open paid audits at a time during pilot. Pause payment link if capacity is reached.
