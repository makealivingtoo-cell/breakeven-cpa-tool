## Qualification rules
A prospect is qualified only when:
- Home-service business serves Saskatoon / nearby market.
- Public website exists and exposes an observable conversion or messaging issue.
- The recipient address is conspicuously published by the business itself, with no nearby prohibition on unsolicited commercial email, OR another lawful consent basis is documented.
- Message is directly relevant to the recipient's business role.
- Outreach includes sender identification and unsubscribe instructions.
- Evidence URL + date are logged before sending.
