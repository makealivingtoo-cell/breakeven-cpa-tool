# Outreach Playbook — Pilot

## Rule
No bulk blast. Every first-touch message must reference one verified public issue and use a recipient whose consent basis is documented.

## First-touch email structure
Subject: Quick website issue I noticed

Hi [Name],

I was reviewing [Business]’s public customer journey and noticed one specific issue: [VERIFIED ISSUE].

I put together three quick fixes:
1. [fix]
2. [fix]
3. [fix]

If useful, I also do a fixed-scope Lead Recovery Snapshot for C$99. It covers the website inquiry path, local/AI visibility, trust signals, CTA copy, and a quote follow-up sequence. No retainer and no access to private customer data required.

[Sender legal/business name]
[Mailing address or other CASL-compliant contact details]
[Phone/email]
If you do not want business emails from me, reply “unsubscribe” and I will stop.

## Follow-up
One follow-up maximum during pilot, only when consent basis remains valid and no unsubscribe/complaint has been received.

## Prohibited
- No invented traffic/revenue claims.
- No “your website is costing you $X” unless using customer-provided numbers and clearly marking it as a scenario.
- No fake form submissions or mystery shopping.
- No “I found you on Google” generic templates.
- No sending where consent basis is uncertain.
