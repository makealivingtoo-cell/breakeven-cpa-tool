# Lead Recovery Snapshot — operating manual
Started: 2026-10-24 | currency CAD | cap: CAD 50
Offer: C$99 one-time 48-hour home-service lead-recovery snapshot after complete intake.
Not yet launched: checkout and intake must be verified, and hosting must be live.
Do not accept funds until fulfillment has been tested with a real public website, payment reconciliation is working, and a contact channel is in place.

## Product boundary
Public website and business-profile review only, no private analytics, call recordings or login access.
Deliver a six-part report: public scorecard, five evidenced conversion frictions, home-page/CTA fixes, three quote follow-up templates, local visibility recommendations, 30-day plan.
No lead volume, conversion lift, search ranking, or revenue claim without source data. Distinguish scenario estimates from verified outcomes. Correct inaccuracies freely.

## Intake
Business name, website, service city/area, three core services, ideal job, preferred reply email, current lead routes and quoted turnaround (optional).
Reply within one business day, deliver within 48 hours of complete intake and confirmed payment, or proactively refund if deadline cannot be met. Do not take orders exceeding capacity.

## Delivery QA
1. Capture review date, actual checked URLs and page excerpts; mark unavailable/unverified elements.
2. Check contact CTA prominence, click-to-call, accessible mobile inquiry flow, required fields, local service pages, trust proof, quote follow-up friction.
3. Prioritize by impact/effort and attach evidence with exact URL.
4. Include actionable replacements: two CTA variants, homepage intro copy, three follow-up messages.
5. Avoid claiming rankings or access to private operational results.
6. Proofread, verify links, send PDF to customer, log delivery timestamp and refund risk.

## Privacy + support
Store minimum customer data; do not commit personal data to public GitHub.
Use private, access-controlled service records for inquiries and delivery. No user-provided credentials.
Acknowledge support within one business day. Refund or correct incorrect undelivered work; never stonewall.
No mass cold email. Canadian CASL requires a documented legal basis and sender identification/contact/unsubscribe for covered commercial messages.
For outbound, record public relevance, consent basis, date, and suppression; never automate bulk sending.

## Safety / finance
Zero recurring costs and no paid hosting without a separate budget entry.
Do not deduct pending settlement as profit.
Profit = settled collections - processing fees - refunds - paid software - fulfillment expenses - accrued applicable liabilities.
Reserve full refund exposure until delivery accepted, plus processing fees and obligations.
Max one trial paid deliverable at a time until time/cost are measured.
Pause switch: set STATUS=PAUSED in journal, stop new intake, preserve order obligations and issue needed refunds.
Never send the same commercial email twice: one canonical recipient + campaign identifier, sent timestamp, and status.
Retries: 1 only for transient errors, no repeat payment creation or outreach after unclear result.
Daily audit: settled payments, refunds, support backlog, uptime, and budget; report via automation when feasible.
