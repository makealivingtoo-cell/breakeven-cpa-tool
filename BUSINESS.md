# Lead Recovery Snapshot

## Offer
A fixed-price, 48-hour public-funnel audit for home-service businesses.

**Pilot price:** C$99 one-time for the first 5 paid customers.

## Scope
- Public website conversion review
- Local-search / AI-discoverability review using public signals
- Inquiry-path friction review
- Quote follow-up sequence
- Homepage and CTA rewrite recommendations
- 30-day prioritized action plan

## Guardrails
- No fake leads, impersonation, or deceptive mystery shopping.
- No access to customer/private systems without explicit permission.
- No revenue guarantees. Any revenue-at-risk number is a customer-input scenario.
- Do not accept payment unless intake and 48-hour fulfillment capacity are available.
- Refund immediately if delivery cannot be completed to the stated scope.

## Pilot success rule
Continue after the first 20 qualified outreaches only if we get at least one of:
- 2 qualified replies, or
- 1 paid order / purchase attempt.

Otherwise revise positioning, niche, or channel before another outreach batch.
